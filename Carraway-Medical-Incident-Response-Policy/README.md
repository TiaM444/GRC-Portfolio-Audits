Incident Response Policy: Carraway Medical Center (CMC)



📌 Project Overview

This project involved developing a comprehensive Incident Response Policy for Carraway Medical Center (CMC). The goal was to establish a standardized framework for identifying, assessing, and responding to security incidents while ensuring the protection of sensitive healthcare data.


🛡️ Regulatory & Industry Alignment

The policy was authored to meet strict healthcare security standards and federal guidelines:

HIPAA Security Rule: Specifically addressing Security Incident Procedures (45 CFR 164.308(a)(6)).


NIST SP 800-53: Mapped to the IR (Incident Response) control family, including Incident Handling, Monitoring, and Reporting .


ISO/IEC 27001: Aligned with international standards for incident management planning and evidence collection .

🗝️ Key Features

Severity & Priority Framework: Created a dual-classification system (L1–L4) to distinguish between technical severity and business/patient safety priority.


Cross-Functional Roles: Defined specific responsibilities for the Incident Response Team (IRT), Legal/Compliance, HR, and Public Relations.


Reporting Infrastructure: Established 24/7 reporting channels and specialized protocols for incidents involving Protected Health Information (PHI).


Asset Protection: Focused on the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability) to minimize operational outages and data breaches.
