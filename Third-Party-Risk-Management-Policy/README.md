Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Policy Development


Project Overview

This project features a comprehensive Vendor Management Policy developed for the Duke University Health System (DUHS). The policy establishes a structured framework for managing third-party risks, ensuring the protection of Protected Health Information (PHI) and maintaining compliance with healthcare regulations.


Key Components Developed

Risk-Based Tiering Framework: Established a three-tier classification system (Critical, Moderate, Low) to determine the level of oversight required based on data access and operational impact.

Due Diligence Standards: Defined mandatory pre-onboarding requirements, including the review of SOC reports, security policies, and incident response capabilities.


Regulatory Alignment: Ensured the policy meets standards for HIPAA, HITECH, NIST 800-53, and ISO 27001.

Lifecycle Management: Outlined formal procedures for vendor request initiation, formal approval by the CISO, ongoing monitoring, and secure offboarding/data destruction.


Governance & Accountability

The policy defines clear roles for:

Audit and Compliance Team: Overseeing policy adherence and risk assessments.

Information Security/SOC: Monitoring vendor-related security threats.

Privacy/Compliance Officer: Approving Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and ensuring HIPAA compliance.
